Frequent questions

My screens are empty. Meridiem reads what your Apple Watch records into Health. Wear the watch to bed; the first score arrives the next morning. If you already have nights in Health, check the permission: Health app → Sharing → Apps and Services → Meridiem.

Why does it ask for permissions one by one? Each one is for a single feature, and iOS lets an app ask only once. You → Permissions shows all of them — including the ones on your watch — and what to do if one is off.

Alar-ME did not ring. Leave Meridiem open on the Night Stand before you sleep, and don't close it from the app switcher. Alar-ME keeps nets behind it: when it wakes you with the watch only, the iPhone sounds if the watch has not been answered in ten minutes, and a system alarm rings five minutes after that. With Meridiem in front, the watch taps on its own; if the watch shows its own alert with Open and Stop, that alert's Stop only snoozes — it never ends the morning.

Walking did not stop the alarm. Motion & Fitness has to be on — on the iPhone, and on the watch for the watch alarm (You → Permissions).

Night Stand does not turn sideways. Check that Portrait Orientation Lock is off in Control Center.

I'm changing iPhone, or I deleted the app. A new iPhone restored from your backup brings Meridiem with it — reconnect syncThesis.IO, which stays on the old one on purpose. To move without a backup, or to come back after deleting the app: You › Your data › Save a copy, and on the other iPhone Restore from a copy. Your nights come back from Health on their own.

How do I delete my data? You › Your data › Delete everything Meridiem keeps. Deleting the app from your iPhone and Apple Watch also deletes everything it kept. Health data stays in Health.

Is this medical advice? No. Meridiem is for general wellness and does not diagnose any condition.
