Questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?

Meridiem reads sleep and heart data from Health, which your watch records. Alar-ME and Night Stand work on the iPhone too, and Breathe levels 1 to 3 need only the phone.

Is Meridiem a medical device?

No. Meridiem is for general wellness. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to your doctor about your sleep and your therapy.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone and your watch. There is no Meridiem account. The only connection is syncThesis.IO, if you choose it.

What happens after the free trial?

Advanced renews through the App Store unless you cancel at least a day before the trial ends. Free keeps working, with your last 30 nights.
