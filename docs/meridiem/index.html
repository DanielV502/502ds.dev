Meridiem

Your nights. Wake gently.

Meridiem reads the sleep and heart data your Apple Watch already records, and turns it into something you can act on — measured against your own nights, not against other people.

  • Today — one score for your nights, and the four signals of the night against your usual.
  • Sleep — one night in detail, scored in five parts against your own history.
  • Alar-ME — an alarm that starts quiet, rises slowly and stops once you walk; or a silent tap on your wrist.
  • Breathe — guided breathing toward six breaths a minute, with your heart measured on the watch.
  • Recovery and Move — heart rate variability and steps, against your usual.

Private by design. No account and no analytics. Nothing leaves your devices unless you connect a service yourself.

Meridiem is for general wellness. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.
