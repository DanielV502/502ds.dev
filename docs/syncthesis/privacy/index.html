syncThesis.IO — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026 Service: syncThesis.IO Responsible for your data: Jose Daniel Valenzuela Morales, operating as 502 Design Studio · Guatemala · privacy@syncthesis.io Data protection officer: Jose Daniel Valenzuela Morales, until a data protection officer is designated · privacy@syncthesis.io

The short version

syncThesis.IO keeps the data of breathing therapy and sleep studies — what your therapy machine, sleep study or oximeter recorded, and the clinical record your care team keeps with you — so that you and the clinicians who follow your therapy can read it together. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared without your permission. Your data is stored encrypted on a server in Thailand. You can download it, delete it, or close your account; when you close it, what identifies you is deleted within 30 days.

Who can use syncThesis.IO

Access is by invitation. An account is created for you by the care organization that follows your therapy, or by us when you use syncThesis.IO on your own, or by you from an app you use (for example, Meridiem) when the organization behind that app has invited your email address. Creating your own account is open to adults in Guatemala and Thailand; other countries are added one at a time.

What we keep

Your account. Your name, email address and role (patient, clinician, or an organization's staff), the organization you belong to, and the clinicians assigned to you.

Signing in. A one-time code and your authenticator app (TOTP) — there are no passwords. Codes and session tokens are stored only as hashes. When you create your account, we note the country your network address belongs to beside the country you declared — the country only, never the address — to check that accounts come from the countries where the service is available; it is worked out on our own server with DB-IP's free database (db-ip.com), and nothing is sent to anyone. We record each sign-in and the network address it came from, to protect your account from repeated attempts and to keep the audit record described below.

Health data, from your devices and your care.

  • Therapy machines (PAP): when the machine was used, its pressures, leaks, breathing rate and volumes, the breathing events it recorded, and its waveforms.
  • Sleep studies (PSG) and oximetry (SpO₂): the recordings and the measures computed from them.
  • Wearables you connect yourself (for example, Fitbit or Apple Watch data through Google Health): only what you authorize in their own consent screen, and only until you disconnect.
  • Your clinical record: laboratory results, studies, diagnoses and treatments, entered and reviewed by your care team or by you.
  • Your devices: their make, model and serial number. The serial number is how a file is recognized as coming from your machine.

The figures syncThesis.IO computes — the apnea-hypopnea index, leak, adherence, a night's therapy score — are estimates for you and your clinician to review together. They do not diagnose any condition.

How it is used

To show your data to you and to the clinicians who follow your therapy; to compute the measures above; to keep the service secure and working. Nothing else. There is no advertising and no third-party analytics. To know which parts of the service are used, it counts each screen opened with the role of whoever opened it (patient, clinician) — never who it was, and never anything about your health.

Why we are allowed to use it

  • Your health data: your explicit consent, which you give when you create your account and can withdraw by deleting your data or closing your account.
  • Your account and sign-in: what is needed to give you the service you asked for.
  • The sign-in log and the audit record: our duty to keep your data secure and to prove every access was legitimate.
  • Answering your requests about your data: the law that gives you those rights.

Who can see it

  • You, always.
  • The clinicians assigned to you in your care organization.
  • Anyone else with a role in that organization sees you only under a code, never by name.
  • The operator of the service, only to run, support and secure it. Any access to a real name outside the care relationship is a recorded, time-limited exception.
  • An app you connect (for example, Meridiem), through Partner Connect: only what you grant on syncThesis.IO's own consent screen, under a pseudonym made for that app alone, until you withdraw it. In My account you see every connected app, what it may read, and each device it is connected from — what the device is, the network address it last connected from, and when — so you can withdraw one device's connection or the whole app.

We do not sell, rent or give your data to anyone else.

Research, only if you say yes

Today syncThesis.IO does not use your data for research or platform statistics. When it does, it will ask you first, with a separate question at sign-up and in your account — "Yes, contribute" or "No, thanks", neither chosen for you. Answering no changes nothing in the service. If you say yes:

  • A de-identified copy of your measures joins a research set: no name, no email, no serial number (each device becomes "make, model, #1"), no documents or free text, and your dates shifted by a random amount known only to your private key — the time of day, the day of the week and the time between nights are kept, so the story of the therapy can be studied without the calendar dates that could point to you.
  • While your account exists, that copy is linked to you only through your private key, so you can withdraw your yes at any time and your rows are removed.
  • When you close your account, your key is destroyed: the copy can no longer be linked to anyone and stays only as anonymous research data.

Where it is kept, and how

On a server in Thailand, on an encrypted disk, reachable only through encrypted connections. Backup copies are encrypted before they leave the server, with a key that is not kept on it, and copied to equipment under our control.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and your health data: for as long as your account exists.
  • After you close your account or delete your data: removed from the service within 30 days, and from the encrypted backups within 35 days after that.
  • The audit record — who signed in or looked at which record, when, and with what permission; never your health values — is kept for 5 years, to show that every access was legitimate.

Your choices

From My account (your name, at the top right of syncThesis.IO), or by writing to privacy@syncthesis.io:

  • Download your data: what we keep about you — your measures as spreadsheet files (CSV), in the health-data standard FHIR where a mapping exists, and the files you or your care team uploaded.
  • Delete your data: the health data is erased and your account stays, to start again or to remove one device's history. You confirm with the code from your authenticator app; it is erased 30 days after you ask, and you can cancel it until then.
  • Close your account: you confirm with the code from your authenticator app, and you see the exact date of the deletion and how to cancel it. For 30 days the account is locked — no device uploads and no connected apps — and signing in again cancels the closure. If a care organization follows your therapy, it is told, so it can keep in its own records what the law requires it to. On day 30 your account, your private key and everything that identifies you are deleted, and we confirm it to you.

You can also ask us to correct what is wrong, or to tell you what we keep and why. We answer within 30 days.

Other services involved

  • Hosting: the server's provider, in Thailand, which runs the server and is given no access to the service or to your data.
  • Wearables: Google, only if you connect your Fitbit or Apple Watch data through Google Health; their own policy applies to what they hold.
  • Email from us — sign-in codes and the notices of your data requests — is sent through Postmark, an email delivery service in the United States, which handles your address and the message to deliver it. Your health data never go to it. Sending these emails is necessary to give you the service, and Postmark is bound by data processing terms that include the standard contractual clauses for transfers abroad.
  • Email to our contact addresses is forwarded by our domain provider.

Children

To create an account you must have reached the age of majority in your country (20 in Thailand, 18 in Guatemala). Accounts for minors, with a parent's or guardian's consent, are not available yet. If you believe a minor's data is here, write to privacy@syncthesis.io and we will remove it and tell the parent or guardian.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date before the change takes effect; if it changes what is done with your data, we tell you first.

If something goes wrong

If your data are exposed in a way that puts you at high risk, we tell you without delay, with what happened and what you can do, and we tell the data protection authority within 72 hours.

Contact, and your right to complain

privacy@syncthesis.io. You can also complain to the data protection authority: in Thailand, the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee.
