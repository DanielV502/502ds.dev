Last updated: November 25, 2026

The service. syncThesis.IO keeps the data of breathing therapy and sleep studies — what your therapy machine, sleep study or oximeter recorded, and the clinical record your care team keeps with you — so that you and the clinicians who follow you can read it together. It is in a pilot: access is by invitation, and creating your own account is open to adults in the countries listed in the Privacy Policy.
  2. Health information, not medical care. The measures syncThesis.IO computes — the apnea-hypopnea index, leak, adherence, a night's therapy score — are estimates from your devices' own records, for you and your clinician to review together. They do not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or condition, and they are not a substitute for professional medical advice. Decisions about your care stay with you and your clinicians. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
